---------------------------------------
1. Все записи данных заемщиков с возрастом и регионом по убыванию возраста.

credit:PersonalData(?profile) ^
credit:ageYears(?profile, ?age) ^
credit:residenceRegion(?profile, ?region)
-> sqwrl:select(?profile, ?age, ?region) ^
sqwrl:orderByDescending(?age)
---------------------------------------
2. Общее количество записей данных заемщиков.

credit:PersonalData(?profile) ^
sqwrl:makeSet(?profiles, ?profile) ^
sqwrl:size(?count, ?profiles)
-> sqwrl:select(?count)
---------------------------------------
3. Количество записей данных заемщиков по семейному положению.

credit:PersonalData(?profile) ^
credit:maritalStatus(?profile, ?status) ^
sqwrl:makeSet(?profiles, ?profile) ^
sqwrl:groupBy(?profiles, ?status) ^
sqwrl:size(?count, ?profiles)
-> sqwrl:select(?status, ?count) ^
sqwrl:orderByDescending(?count)
---------------------------------------
4. Средний совокупный месячный доход по записям доходов.

credit:IncomeEmployment(?income) ^
credit:monthlyIncome(?income, ?amount) ^
sqwrl:makeBag(?amounts, ?amount) ^
sqwrl:avg(?average, ?amounts)
-> sqwrl:select(?average)
---------------------------------------
5. Профили с хотя бы одной указанной просрочкой.

credit:CreditworthinessAssessment(?a) ^
credit:hasPersonalData(?a, ?profile) ^
credit:hasCreditHistory(?a, ?history) ^
credit:hasPaymentHistory(?history, ?payments) ^
credit:overdueCount(?payments, ?count) ^
swrlb:greaterThan(?count, 0)
-> sqwrl:select(?profile, ?count) ^
sqwrl:orderByDescending(?count)
---------------------------------------
6. Профили с запрашиваемым кредитом на ремонт или покупку мебели.

credit:CreditworthinessAssessment(?a) ^
credit:hasPersonalData(?a, ?profile) ^
credit:hasRequestedLoan(?a, ?loan) ^
credit:loanPurpose(?loan, ?purpose) ^
credit:requestedAmount(?loan, ?amount) ^
swrlb:matches(?purpose, ".*(ремонт|мебел).*")
-> sqwrl:select(?profile, ?purpose, ?amount)
---------------------------------------
7. Профили с положительным дополнительным месячным доходом.

credit:CreditworthinessAssessment(?a) ^
credit:hasPersonalData(?a, ?profile) ^
credit:hasIncomeEmployment(?a, ?income) ^
credit:additionalMonthlyIncome(?income, ?additional) ^
credit:monthlyIncome(?income, ?total) ^
swrlb:greaterThan(?additional, 0)
-> sqwrl:select(?profile, ?additional, ?total) ^
sqwrl:orderByDescending(?additional)
---------------------------------------
8. DTI из месячных долговых платежей и месячного дохода в долях и процентах.

credit:CreditworthinessAssessment(?a) ^
credit:hasPersonalData(?a, ?profile) ^
credit:hasIncomeEmployment(?a, ?income) ^
credit:hasCreditHistory(?a, ?history) ^
credit:hasCurrentCreditLoad(?history, ?load) ^
credit:monthlyIncome(?income, ?amount) ^
credit:monthlyDebtPayments(?load, ?payments) ^
swrlb:greaterThan(?amount, 0) ^
swrlb:multiply(?scaledPayments, ?payments, 1000000.000000) ^
swrlb:divide(?scaledRatio, ?scaledPayments, ?amount) ^
swrlb:add(?shifted, ?scaledRatio, 0.5) ^
swrlb:floor(?rounded, ?shifted) ^
swrlb:multiply(?dti, ?rounded, 0.000001) ^
swrlb:multiply(?percent, ?dti, 100)
-> sqwrl:select(?profile, ?amount, ?payments, ?dti, ?percent) ^
sqwrl:orderByDescending(?dti)
---------------------------------------
