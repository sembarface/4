---------------------------------------
1. Баллы и решения всех заемщиков.

credit:CreditworthinessAssessment(?a) ^
credit:hasPersonalData(?a, ?profile) ^
credit:creditScore(?a, ?score) ^
credit:hasCreditDecision(?a, ?decision)
-> sqwrl:select(?profile, ?score, ?decision) ^
sqwrl:orderByDescending(?score)
---------------------------------------
2. Оценки ниже порога и основания.

credit:CreditworthinessAssessment(?a) ^
credit:hasPersonalData(?a, ?profile) ^
credit:creditScore(?a, ?score) ^
credit:approvalThreshold(?a, ?threshold) ^
swrlb:lessThan(?score, ?threshold) ^
credit:hasScoreReason(?a, ?reason)
-> sqwrl:select(?profile, ?score, ?reason) ^
sqwrl:orderByDescending(?score)
---------------------------------------
3. Составляющие итогового балла.

credit:CreditworthinessAssessment(?a) ^
credit:hasPersonalData(?a, ?profile) ^
credit:creditScore(?a, ?score) ^
credit:profilePoints(?a, ?p1) ^
credit:affordabilityPoints(?a, ?p2) ^
credit:incomePoints(?a, ?p3) ^
credit:historyPoints(?a, ?p4) ^
credit:savingsPoints(?a, ?p5) ^
credit:debtPoints(?a, ?p6) ^
credit:riskAdjustment(?a, ?adjustment)
-> sqwrl:select(?profile, ?score, ?p1, ?p2, ?p3, ?p4, ?p5, ?p6, ?adjustment) ^
sqwrl:orderByDescending(?score)
