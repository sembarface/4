---------------------------------------
1. Получить итоговый балл как сумму положительных и отрицательных вкладов.

credit:CreditworthinessAssessment(?a) ^
credit:profilePoints(?a, ?p1) ^
credit:affordabilityPoints(?a, ?p2) ^
credit:incomePoints(?a, ?p3) ^
credit:historyPoints(?a, ?p4) ^
credit:savingsPoints(?a, ?p5) ^
credit:debtPoints(?a, ?p6) ^
credit:riskAdjustment(?a, ?adjustment) ^
swrlb:add(?score, ?p1, ?p2, ?p3, ?p4, ?p5, ?p6, ?adjustment)
-> credit:creditScore(?a, ?score)
---------------------------------------
2. Выдать кредит, если итоговый балл не ниже порога.

credit:creditScore(?a, ?score) ^
credit:approvalThreshold(?a, ?threshold) ^
swrlb:greaterThanOrEqual(?score, ?threshold)
-> credit:hasCreditDecision(?a, credit:GrantCredit)
---------------------------------------
3. Не выдавать кредит, если итоговый балл ниже порога.

credit:creditScore(?a, ?score) ^
credit:approvalThreshold(?a, ?threshold) ^
swrlb:lessThan(?score, ?threshold)
-> credit:hasCreditDecision(?a, credit:DoNotGrantCredit)
---------------------------------------
